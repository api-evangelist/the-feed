# The Feed

> The Feed is an online sports nutrition store for endurance athletes. It stocks products from more than 300 brands covering fuel, hydration, supplements, and recovery gear.

Matt Johnson founded The Feed while managing a Tour de France cycling team. He observed that better nutrition gave World Tour riders measurable performance gains in training and in competition, and he built The Feed to make that same level of nutritional planning available to athletes at all ability levels.

The company structures fueling around three points in a workout: what to eat before, during, and after. That framework has been tested and adjusted over more than ten years using input from over 200,000 athletes. Individual needs vary by sport, body, diet, and training goals, so The Feed provides tools to build a personalized plan around those factors.

Every product on the site has been taste tested and used during actual training sessions by the team. The Feed favors natural products, rotates inventory every few weeks to keep stock fresh, and carries NSF Certified for Sport products for athletes who require third-party verification. The store works with both large companies like Clif Bar and small producers like Vafels, and it keeps only products that pass internal quality and taste standards.

What separates The Feed from other retailers:

- **Single serving purchases.** Most stores sell gels, chews, and drink mixes by the box. The Feed sells them individually. Athletes can test different products and flavors without committing to full quantities, and they save money by ordering only what they need.
- **More than 300 brands.** The catalog includes Maurten, Skratch Labs, GU Energy, Clif Bar, Honey Stinger, and hundreds of other brands, with more than 15 sold exclusively through The Feed.
- **Free standard shipping on all US orders.** FedEx 2-Day and Overnight are available at additional cost.
- **AutoShip subscriptions.** Recurring orders save up to 15%, with delivery intervals from weekly to every three months. Useful for keeping nutrition fresh and rotating through flavors.
- **Feed First membership.** A loyalty program with ongoing savings and member perks.
- **Professional athlete partnerships.** The Feed is the official nutrition partner of USA Triathlon. Sponsored athletes include ultrarunner Courtney Dauwalter and gravel cyclist Pete Stetina, among others.
- **4.5 stars on Trustpilot** from more than 2,000 verified customer reviews. Customers consistently cite fast shipping and accurate order fulfillment.
- **Social responsibility.** The Feed partners with Ride For Racial Justice and Black Girls Do Bike. Fulfillment uses custom-cut boxes sized to each order to reduce waste, and all packing documentation is handled electronically.
